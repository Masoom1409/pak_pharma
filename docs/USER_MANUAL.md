# PharmaCore — Short User Manual

1. **Login:** Enter your PIN (Owner 1234 / Cashier 1111 on first run). Change it after first login.
2. **New bill (POS):** Click POS, scan a barcode or type a name and press Enter. Scan again to add more.
   Use F2 New Bill, F3 Search, F5 Pay, F8 Hold, F9 Recall. Expired medicines are blocked automatically.
3. **Units:** Change tablet/strip/box on a cart line — the price and stock convert automatically.
4. **Discount:** Set bill discount % in the Payment panel. Cashiers above the limit need a manager PIN.
5. **Stock-in by scanning:** Open Scan to Add, scan items, edit batch/expiry/qty, then Confirm Stock In.
   Unknown barcode → "New medicine" popup, barcode is prefilled.
6. **Alerts:** The bell shows expiring (≤90 days), expired and low-stock items. Red = ≤30 days, orange = ≤90.
7. **Customers (udhaar):** Attach a customer on a bill, choose Credit, and the balance goes to their ledger.
8. **Backup:** Settings → Create backup now. A backup is also taken when the app closes (Electron).
9. **Reports:** Reports → choose dates → Load → Export CSV. Bills are never deleted, only voided with a reason.
10. **Offline:** Everything works without internet. WhatsApp sharing only opens a wa.me link when online.
